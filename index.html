          Quick anchors
          The hook
          Read out loud before every session. Lock the rhythm in your mouth before they ask "tell me about yourself."
          "I'm an engineer who switched from rocks to networks. My undergrad was geological engineering at KNUST — instruments, safety, root-cause discipline. Over the last two years I added the digital layer at Pace, GPA 3.86. Deploy Tech is where my two worlds meet — hands on the cable, instruments in hand, and because I'm Linux/security too, I can troubleshoot what I deploy."
          Story DNA
          5 non-negotiable ingredients of a great LP story. Mentally check off as you draft.
          • Name a person — "Sarah from compliance", not "the team"
          • One quantified outcome — number, %, $, time, scale
          • One honest gap — what you'd do differently
          • One bridge — explicitly name the LP at the end
          • One warmth moment — a feeling, a reaction, a quote
          Story sources
          Pick a story you've actually lived for each LP. Don't fabricate — interviewers can smell it. Vary across interviewers; never reuse a story.
          • KNUST SRC VP COVID — Ownership · Bias for Action · Earn Trust
          • National Grid PCI DSS — Customer Obsession · Earn Trust
          • Anomaly Detection capstone — Dive Deep · Highest Standards
          • Network IR Lab — Deliver Results · Dive Deep
          • Pace AI Lab TA — Highest Standards · Hire & Develop
          • Geological eng identity — Learn & Be Curious · Ownership
          Hard rules for the room
          Glance at these between questions. The last rule is the disqualifier — say it once, you're out.
          • Ask clarifying questions before diving in
          • Always talk about customers
          • Think out loud — show how you reason
          • Quantify everything you can
          • Be vocally self-critical when relevant
          • Never say "that's not my job"
          Gap reframes
          Memorize the lines. When the interviewer pushes on a missing qualification, these are the exact words.
          • No DC experience? → "Pace network labs + 5 years of geological field sites — same playbook: instruments, SOPs, document."
          • No CCNA/A+? → "LPI Linux × 2 + Security+. CCNA on my summer plan."
          • No OTDR hands-on? → "I know the theory; analogous instruments in geological surveys. Day-one supervised, then signed off."
          • BGP/OSPF? → "Fundamentals — path-vector vs link-state. Not production-yet. Reading on day one."
          Why you (the 0.1%)

          • Field-tested engineering discipline — 5 years of geological surveys, safety-critical, instrument-driven
          • Crisis-tested leadership at scale — VP for 60,000+ students through COVID
          • Customer-side empathy from PCI DSS work — rare among technical candidates
          • Bilingual — English fluent + French professional
